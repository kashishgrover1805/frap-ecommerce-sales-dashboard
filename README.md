# FRAP E-Commerce Sales & Performance Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive e-commerce sales analytics dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, revenue trends, product performance, returns, and regional performance.

The dashboard provides an executive-level overview of key business KPIs and helps identify sales patterns and potential growth opportunities.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer key business questions such as:

- What is the overall sales and revenue performance?
- Which products are performing the best?
- Which products are underperforming?
- How is revenue changing month over month?
- Which regions/states contribute the most to revenue?
- What is the average order value?
- What is the return rate?
- Where are potential sales growth opportunities?

## 📌 Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks the following key performance indicators:

- Total Orders
- Units Sold
- Gross Revenue
- Net Revenue
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Return Rate
- Total Discount

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Executive Overview

The dashboard includes:

- KPI cards for key business metrics
- Monthly revenue trend analysis
- Top 10 products by net revenue
- State-wise performance analysis
- Interactive date filtering
- Product and sales performance analysis
- Tooltip-based KPI details

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

## 🔄 Data Preparation

Power Query was used to prepare the raw data for analysis, including:

- Data cleaning and transformation
- Date field preparation
- Product name standardization
- Geographic data preparation
- Preparation of data for Power BI visualizations

## 🧮 DAX Measures

Key DAX measures were created for business KPIs including:

- Total Orders
- Units Sold
- Gross Revenue
- Net Revenue
- Average Order Value
- Return Rate
- Total Discount

## 💡 Business Insights

The dashboard helps identify:

- Top-performing products contributing significantly to revenue
- Low-performing products that may require further analysis
- Monthly sales and revenue trends
- Differences in performance across regions/states
- Product categories and areas with potential growth opportunities
